PINS view

Prototypes exploring what the Planning Inspectorate (PINS) sees of a local plan, from checking its progress before submission to examining it. These are for inspectors rather than LPA staff, so each one uses its own header without the site's internal navigation. Pick one to try it out.

Current prototypes

These are the most recent prototypes we've developed. We'll be testing these over the next few weeks.

Examination - inspector view

What an independent planning inspector sees when examining a submitted local plan.

User need: As a planning inspector, I need to examine a submitted local plan and its supporting evidence to assess whether it is sound and legally compliant.

Gateway 2 progress check

How an independent planning inspector can check the progress of a submitted local plan's chapters and evidence base.

User need: As a planning inspector, I need to see the progress of every chapter and evidence document in a submitted local plan, so I can check it is ready for examination.
